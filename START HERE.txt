Dragon Quest IX for PC
======================

This turns YOUR OWN Dragon Quest IX ROM (dumped from your cartridge; the
USA and European versions work, others can be tried) into a PC version of the game. No game files are included.

1. Double-click "DQIX Launcher.exe" (in the release zip; "dqix-launcher" on
   Linux and the Steam Deck). The first time it asks for your ROM, then builds
   the game and gets the HD pack, with two bars (Linux and Steam Deck: no HD
   pack or updates yet, Windows only for now).
   - The first time it installs free build tools (MSYS2) and takes 10-20 minutes.
   - It needs Windows 10/11 (64-bit), ~4 GB free space and the internet once.
2. PLAY lights up when everything is ready. Open the launcher again to play:
   it updates the game and the HD pack first.
   With shortcuts and an uninstaller instead: "Install Dragon Quest IX.bat".

Without the window: put your ROM (.nds) in the "rom" folder, double-click
BUILD.bat, then play game\dqix.exe.

Controls: arrows = D-pad, X = A, Z = B, S = X, A = Y, Q/W = L/R,
Enter = START, Right Shift = SELECT, mouse = touch screen, Tab = fast forward.
Gamepads work too. Every setting is in the menu bar at the top of the window.

If something goes wrong, press F10 in the game: it saves a bug report in
game\reports\ (with a box to describe what happened). Send that folder.

Please don't share the "game" or "build" folders: they contain the game.
